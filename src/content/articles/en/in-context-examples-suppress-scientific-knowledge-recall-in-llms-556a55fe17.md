The study examined how in-context examples change LLM behavior across 60 latent structure recovery tasks, 6,000 trials, four models, and five scientific domains. A key condition was that some examples were generated by the same formula as the test problems, allowing a direct look at whether models shift from knowledge-driven reasoning to empirical fitting.

Across all conditions, providing in-context examples made models rely less on pretrained domain knowledge and more on empirical pattern fitting. The accuracy impact was mixed: it sometimes lowered accuracy, sometimes left it unchanged, and sometimes appeared to improve it; in all cases, however, model reasoning shifted away from knowledge-driven processing.

The abstract reports aggregate outcomes only. It does not identify which four models were tested or provide effect sizes, so the magnitude of the practical deployment impact is not yet quantified.
