RankByGene is a representation learning method for aligning histopathology images with gene expression data. It uses a ranking-based cross-modal consistency loss and incorporates self-supervised knowledge distillation with a teacher-student architecture as an intra-modal stability regularizer to prevent image-representation drift during alignment. The paper has been accepted by IEEE Transactions on Medical Imaging (TMI) in 2026, and code is publicly available.

Experiments on seven public datasets demonstrate improved alignment and predictive performance over existing methods across gene expression prediction, slide-level classification, and survival analysis.
