Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) systems enhance generation by incorporating external knowledge, and graph-structured GraphRAG methods have gained recent attention. However, fairly evaluating these systems remains challenging. The study identifies two critical flaws in current answer evaluation frameworks: unrelated questions and evaluation biases, which may lead to biased or wrong conclusions.

To address these issues, the researchers propose an unbiased evaluation framework that uses graph-text-grounded question generation and an unbiased evaluation procedure. Applying this framework to three representative GraphRAG methods reveals that their performance gains are much more moderate than previously reported.

The provided abstract does not include specific numerical results or detailed methodology; the full paper is needed for quantitative evidence. Therefore, the exact magnitude of performance gains and the detailed implementation of the evaluation framework require further verification.
