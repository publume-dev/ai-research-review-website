The study introduces a novel interactive theory-of-mind (ToM) evaluation paradigm that requires an agent to persuade a target by strategically revealing information, depending on the target's knowledge and motivational states. Three experiments test the persuasive abilities of large language models (LLMs).

In the Hidden condition of Experiment 1, LLMs performed below chance, suggesting difficulty with multi-step planning to elicit and use mental state information. However, in Experiments 2 and 3, where human targets role-played the bot or real beliefs were measured, LLMs outperformed human persuaders across all conditions.

The study is an arXiv preprint and has not been peer-reviewed. Experiment 3 measures belief change, but details on effect sizes and statistical significance are not fully provided in the abstract.
