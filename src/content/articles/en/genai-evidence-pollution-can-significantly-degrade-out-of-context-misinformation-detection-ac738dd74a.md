The study addresses the risk that generative AI (GenAI) could be used to pollute evidence, misleading out-of-context misinformation detection systems. The paper is a preprint and has not been peer-reviewed; evaluation uses two benchmark datasets.

Experiments show that GenAI-polluted evidence can degrade the performance of state-of-the-art out-of-context misinformation detectors by more than 9 percentage points. To counter this, the authors propose two mitigation strategies: cross-modal evidence reranking and cross-modal claim-evidence reasoning. These strategies enhance the robustness of existing detectors on benchmark datasets.

The study is a preprint and has not been peer-reviewed; specific effect sizes and benchmark dataset details are not fully described in the abstract, so the findings require further validation.
