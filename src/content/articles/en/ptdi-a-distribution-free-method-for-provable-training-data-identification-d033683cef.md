The paper introduces PTDI, a distribution-free method that provides provable and strict false identification rate control for training data identification in large language models.

In experiments across various models and datasets, PTDI achieves higher statistical power than prior methods while strictly controlling the false identification rate.

The paper is an arXiv preprint that has not yet been peer-reviewed, and independent verification and replication are still pending.
