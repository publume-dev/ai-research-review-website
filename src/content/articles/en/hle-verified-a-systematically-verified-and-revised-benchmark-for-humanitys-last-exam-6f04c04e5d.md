HLE-Verified is a verified and revised version of the HLE benchmark, constructed via a two-stage validation-and-repair workflow: Stage I yields 668 verified items, Stage II yields 1,143 revised-and-certified items, and the remaining 689 items are released as an uncertain set.

Evaluation of eight state-of-the-art language models on HLE and HLE-Verified shows an average absolute accuracy gain of 7–10 percentage points on HLE-Verified, with gains of 30–40 percentage points on items with erroneous original problem statements or reference answers. The paper also reports a strong association between model confidence and the presence of errors in the problem statement or reference answer, supporting the effectiveness of the revisions.

The paper is a preprint and has not undergone peer review. The abstract does not provide details on the evaluation setup (e.g., exact models, prompts, or statistical significance), which limits verification of the claimed gains.
